## 📱 The TikTok / Reels / Shorts Playbook

Hyper-casual directional puzzle games generate massive organic virality through **ASMR satisfaction** and **engineered frustration**. You do not need to spend money on paid ads—organic 15-second clips can easily generate tens of thousands of plays.

---

### Formula 1: The "ASMR Brain Scratch" (Pure Satisfaction)

- **Video Length:** 12–15 seconds.
- **Visuals:** Record screen of Level 5 (The Pinwheel) or Level 7 (Tangled Labyrinth) using the **Cyber Neon** or **Gold Obsidian** theme.
- **Action Sequence:**
  1. Start with the congested, jammed grid.
  2. Tap arrows in rapid, rhythmic succession (1 tap every 0.4s).
  3. The combo counter leaps: `1x -> 2x -> 3x -> 4x -> 5x COMBO 🔥`.
  4. Web Audio synthesizer plays ascending musical scale notes in rhythm with taps.
  5. Final tap: Super Magnet clears remaining 4 arrows in a fireworks cascade!
- **On-Screen Text Hook:**
  - *"Tell me this doesn't scratch the back of your brain 🤤"*
  - *"The sound when you clear the whole board in one combo..."*
- **Audio:** Original game audio (turn device volume up so the crystal-clear procedural chimes and whooshes are front and center).
- **Caption:** *"Free in bio (no download needed)! Can you beat Level 8 without using a hammer? #puzzlegame #asmrsounds #satisfying #arrowout #gaming"*

---

### Formula 2: The "Engineered Fail / Only 2% Can Solve" (Comment Rage & Virality)

- **Video Length:** 10–14 seconds.
- **Visuals:** Screen recording of Level 6 (Fortress Gate) or Level 8 (Neon Gridlock).
- **Action Sequence:**
  1. Voiceover or text: *"My girlfriend said this level is literally impossible..."*
  2. The player taps the obvious center arrow first $\to$ **BUZZ! Arrow shakes and flashes red.**
  3. The player tries another blocked arrow $\to$ **BUZZ! Another rejection shake.**
  4. The player taps the Disintegrator Hammer and crushes one arrow, then solves it.
- **On-Screen Text Hook:**
  - *"98% of people tap the wrong arrow first 🤦‍♂️"*
  - *"Level 6 is mathematically rigged... or am I just blind?"*
- **Psychological Trigger:** Viewers will flood the comment section shouting: *"You have to tap the top right arrow first!! Are you blind?!"*
- **Algorithmic Result:** Hundreds of comments signal high engagement to TikTok/Reels algorithms, sending the video onto the For You Page (FYP).

### Formula 3: The "Developer Journey / Indie Hacker"

- **Target Audience:** Indie devs, creators, micro-SaaS builders, Reddit r/webdev, r/SideProject.
- **Action Sequence:**
  - *"I got tired of games with 30-second unskippable video ads, so I built a zero-bloat arrow puzzle game with procedural Web Audio in Next.js 16."*
  - Show the Level Editor creating a custom puzzle in 10 seconds.
  - Show the one-click Gumroad turnkey package export.
